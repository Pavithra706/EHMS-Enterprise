# 1. Introduction

This document provides an overview of the reference hospital for which the Enterprise Hospital Management System (EHMS) is being developed. It describes the hospital's size, operational model, services, departments, staff, and patient categories to establish a clear understanding of the business environment before designing the software.

---

# 2. Hospital Overview

The reference hospital is a large 24×7 super speciality hospital providing comprehensive healthcare services across multiple medical specialties. The hospital offers outpatient, inpatient, emergency, trauma, surgical, diagnostic, pharmacy, and rehabilitation services.

The hospital aims to replace manual and paper-based processes with a fully digital platform that improves patient care, operational efficiency, and decision-making.

---

The hospital is classified as a:

- Multi-disciplinary Healthcare Institution
- Teaching and Training Enabled (Future Scope)

# 10. Operational Challenges

The current hospital environment may face several operational challenges:

- Paper-based documentation
- Delays in patient registration
- Long waiting times
- Manual coordination between departments
- Limited real-time visibility
- Billing inaccuracies
- Inventory tracking difficulties
- Equipment monitoring challenges
- Difficulty generating analytics and reports

---

# 11. Expected Digital Transformation

The implementation of EHMS aims to:

- Digitize all hospital workflows.
- Eliminate paper-based records.
- Maintain a centralized Electronic Health Record (EHR).
- Improve communication between departments.
- Enable real-time patient tracking.
- Improve operational efficiency.
- Strengthen security and auditability.
- Support future scalability and innovation.
