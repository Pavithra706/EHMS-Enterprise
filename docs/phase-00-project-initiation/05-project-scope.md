# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to clearly define the boundaries of the Enterprise Hospital Management System (EHMS). It specifies which business processes, departments, modules, and technical capabilities are included in Version 1.0 and identifies items planned for future releases.

---

# 2. In Scope

EHMS Version 1.0 includes the complete digitization of hospital operations across clinical, administrative, financial, and operational domains.

The system includes:

## Clinical

- OPD Management
- IPD Management
- Emergency Department
- Trauma Department
- ICU
- NICU
- PICU
- Operation Theatre
- Laboratory
- Radiology
- PACS
- Pharmacy
- Blood Bank
- Dialysis
- Electronic Health Records (EHR)

## Administrative

- Reception
- Patient Registration
- Department Management
- Doctor Management
- Nurse Management
- Employee Management
- Attendance
- Shift Scheduling
- Leave Management
- HR
- Payroll

## Financial

- Registration Billing
- OPD Billing
- IPD Billing
- Pharmacy Billing
- Laboratory Billing
- Radiology Billing
- Insurance
- Corporate Billing
- Payment Management

## Operational

- Inventory
- Biomedical Equipment
- Ward Management
- Bed Management
- Ambulance Management
- Visitor Management
- Housekeeping
- Laundry
- Diet Management
- Complaint Management
- Feedback Management

## Security

- Authentication
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Audit Logs
- Encryption
- Device Tracking
- Session Management

## AI Features

- AI Prescription Suggestions
- Drug Interaction Detection
- Disease Prediction Support
- Voice Notes
- Speech-to-Text
- AI Chatbot
- Predictive Analytics

---

# 3. Out of Scope

The following are not included in Version 1.0 but may be considered in future releases:

- National healthcare registry integration
- Wearable medical device integration
- IoT-enabled smart hospital automation
- Robotics integration
- Advanced clinical research platform
- Cross-country healthcare interoperability
- Genomic data management

---

# 4. Hospital Coverage

The system is designed for a:

- 24×7 Super Speciality Hospital
- 80–120 Departments
- 3,000+ Employees
- 10,000+ Patients per Day

Future versions will support multiple hospitals under one organization.
