# 1. Introduction

A super speciality hospital consists of multiple departments working together to provide complete patient care. Each department has specific responsibilities, staff members, resources, and workflows.

This document classifies the departments and summarizes their roles in hospital operations.

---

# 2. Department Classification

The hospital departments are grouped into four major categories:

- Clinical Departments
- Diagnostic Departments
- Support Departments
- Administrative Departments

---

# 7. Department Communication

Hospital departments continuously exchange information.

Typical interactions include:

- Reception → OPD
- OPD → Laboratory
- OPD → Radiology
- Laboratory → Doctor
- Radiology → Doctor
- Doctor → Pharmacy
- Pharmacy → Billing
- Billing → Insurance
- Emergency → ICU
- ICU → Operation Theatre
- Operation Theatre → CSSD

Efficient communication between departments is essential for reducing delays and improving patient care.
