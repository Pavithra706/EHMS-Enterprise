# 1. Introduction

The Enterprise Hospital Management System (EHMS) aims to provide a unified, secure, and intelligent digital platform for managing all clinical, administrative, financial, and operational activities of a 24×7 super speciality hospital.

The objectives defined in this document will guide the design, development, testing, deployment, and future enhancement of the system.

---

# 2. Primary Objectives

- Digitize all hospital workflows.
- Eliminate paper-based documentation.
- Maintain a single Electronic Health Record (EHR) for every patient.
- Improve communication between hospital departments.
- Reduce patient waiting time.
- Improve operational efficiency.
- Support data-driven decision-making.

---

# 3. Clinical Objectives

The system shall:

- Maintain complete Electronic Health Records (EHR).
- Support OPD and IPD workflows.
- Manage Emergency and Trauma cases.
- Support ICU, NICU, and PICU operations.
- Provide digital prescriptions.
- Integrate Laboratory Information System (LIS).
- Integrate Radiology Information System (RIS).
- Support PACS for medical images.
- Enable barcode and QR-based patient identification.
- Provide clinical decision support through AI (future phase).

---

# 4. Administrative Objectives

The system shall:

- Manage hospital departments.
- Manage doctors, nurses, and employees.
- Track attendance and shifts.
- Manage room and bed allocation.
- Manage visitor records.
- Manage housekeeping and maintenance.
- Manage biomedical equipment.
- Generate operational reports and dashboards.

---

# 5. Financial Objectives

The system shall:

- Generate accurate bills.
- Support OPD and IPD billing.
- Process insurance claims.
- Support corporate billing.
- Record payments and refunds.
- Generate financial reports.
- Reduce billing errors.

---

# 6. Technical Objectives

The system shall:

- Be cloud-native.
- Support 24×7 operation.
- Scale to more than 10,000 patients per day.
- Provide real-time synchronization.
- Expose secure REST APIs.
- Support modular architecture.
- Be deployable using Docker and Kubernetes.
- Maintain high availability and fault tolerance.

---

# 7. Security Objectives

The system shall:

- Implement Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
- Support Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Encrypt sensitive data.
- Maintain audit logs.
- Secure APIs using JWT.
- Track user sessions and devices.
- Support regular backups and disaster recovery.

---

# 8. AI Objectives

Future AI capabilities include:

- AI-assisted prescription suggestions.
- Drug interaction detection.
- Disease prediction support.
- Medical image analysis.
- Voice-to-text clinical documentation.
- Predictive bed occupancy.
- Medicine stock forecasting.
- Intelligent healthcare chatbot.

---

# 9. Long-Term Objectives

EHMS is designed to evolve into a Digital Hospital Platform capable of supporting:

- Multi-hospital deployment.
- Multi-city healthcare networks.
- Telemedicine.
- National healthcare integrations.
- Advanced analytics.
- AI-powered healthcare services.
- Smart hospital automation.

---

# 10. Success Metrics

The project will be considered successful if it achieves:

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Paper Usage | Reduced by 95% or more |
| Daily Patient Capacity | 10,000+ |
| Registration Time | Less than 2 minutes |
| Patient Record Retrieval | Less than 2 seconds |
| System Availability | 99.9% |
| API Response Time | Less than 300 ms (typical) |
| Billing Accuracy | Greater than 99% |
| Electronic Record Coverage | 100% of registered patients |
