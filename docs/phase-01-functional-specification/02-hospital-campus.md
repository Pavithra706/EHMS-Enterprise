# 1. Introduction

The Enterprise Hospital Management System (EHMS) is designed for a modern super speciality hospital campus that operates 24×7. The hospital campus is planned to ensure efficient movement of patients, staff, ambulances, visitors, and medical supplies while maintaining safety and operational efficiency.

---
