# 1. Introduction

The Enterprise Hospital Management System (EHMS) is designed for a modern 24×7 super speciality hospital that provides comprehensive healthcare services through fully digital workflows. The hospital eliminates paper-based processes by adopting electronic health records, cloud storage, barcode-based patient tracking, and real-time communication between departments.

The system is intended to support high patient volumes while maintaining efficiency, security, and quality of care.

---

# 2. Vision

To build a fully digital, paperless, cloud-enabled hospital capable of delivering high-quality healthcare through secure, scalable, and intelligent technology.

---

# 3. Hospital Profile

The hospital is a multi-speciality, tertiary care institution operating 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Key characteristics:

- Super speciality healthcare
- 80–120 departments
- OPD and IPD services
- Emergency and Trauma Care
- Intensive Care Units
- Operation Theatres
- Laboratory and Radiology
- Pharmacy
- Blood Bank
- Telemedicine
- AI-assisted clinical decision support
- Cloud-based Electronic Health Records

---

# 4. Hospital Objectives

The hospital aims to:

- Deliver quality patient care.
- Minimize waiting time.
- Eliminate paperwork.
- Improve communication across departments.
- Maintain accurate electronic medical records.
- Enhance patient safety.
- Improve operational efficiency.
- Support evidence-based clinical decisions.
- Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations.

# 6. Operational Model

The hospital operates continuously (24×7) with multiple shifts for clinical and non-clinical staff.

Core operational principles:

- Digital-first workflow
- Real-time data synchronization
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Barcode and QR code patient identification
- Integrated departmental communication
- Cloud-based document storage
- Continuous monitoring and audit logging

| Operation Theatres | 20+ |

These values are planning estimates and may be adjusted during detailed design.
